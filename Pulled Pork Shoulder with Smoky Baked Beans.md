# Pulled Pork Shoulder with Smoky Baked Beans (Kamado)

## Meat
- 2–2.5kg boneless pork shoulder (British cut — ask the butcher for shoulder with a decent fat cap; if buying supermarket, a rolled shoulder joint works, just remove the netting and open it out)

## Pork Rub
- 2 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp fine sea salt
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp mustard powder

## Baked Beans
- 400g dried haricot beans (soaked overnight) or 3 × 400g tins, drained
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 100g smoked bacon lardons
- 500ml passata
- 2 tbsp black treacle
- 1 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cider vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp English mustard
- 250ml chicken stock (more if using dried beans)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

### Prep (night before or morning of)
1. Pat the pork dry, trim any loose flaps, and apply the rub all over. Fridge overnight if possible, minimum 1 hour.
2. If using dried beans, soak overnight, then simmer for 45–60 minutes until just tender before the cook. Tinned beans skip this.

### Kamado Setup
3. Set up for indirect with the heat deflector in. Target 120–130°C dome temp. A couple of chunks of oak or apple wood.
4. Pork on the grate, probe in the thickest part.

### The Cook
5. Run at 120–130°C. Expect roughly 1.5–2 hours per kg, but cook to temp, not time.
6. **Beans on at the 2-hour mark**: fry the lardons and onion in a Dutch oven (do this on the hob or over the coals before the deflector goes in — or just use raw and let the smoker do the work). Add everything else, stir, and place the uncovered Dutch oven under or beside the pork so it catches drippings and takes on smoke.
7. Stir the beans every hour or so. If they thicken too much, loosen with stock. Lid on for the last stretch if they're where you want them.
8. **Wrap the pork at the stall** (~70°C internal) in butcher's paper or foil.
9. Pull the pork at **93–95°C internal**. It should probe like butter.

### Rest and Serve
10. Rest the pork, wrapped, in a cool box or warm oven (off) for at least 1 hour.
11. Pull, mixing the bark through. Season the beans, add a splash of vinegar if they need lifting.
12. Serve with soft white rolls and slaw.

## Timing Guide (2kg shoulder)
| Stage | Approx. time |
|---|---|
| Kamado to temp | 45 min |
| Unwrapped cook to stall | 4–5 hrs |
| Wrapped to 93–95°C | 2–3 hrs |
| Rest | 1 hr+ |
| **Total** | **8–10 hrs** |
